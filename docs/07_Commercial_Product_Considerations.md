# Commercial Product Considerations

## Positioning

PC Repair Toolkit is a Windows diagnostic, maintenance and
troubleshooting application for home users, PC enthusiasts and
independent technicians/repair businesses. Its differentiator should be
technical capability paired with understandable explanations.

Avoid unsupported claims such as "fixes every PC problem", "guaranteed
repairs" or "detects all hardware faults".

## Possible editions

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  Edition                 Audience                Possible scope
  ----------------------- ----------------------- -----------------------
  Personal                Everyday users          Guided scans,
                                                  explanations, selected
                                                  safe maintenance and
                                                  basic reports

  Advanced                Enthusiasts             Detailed diagnostics,
                                                  expanded analysis and
                                                  advanced controls

  Technician              Repair businesses       Professional reports,
                                                  device/session
                                                  workflow, technician
                                                  notes and advanced
                                                  diagnostics
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

These are options, not final pricing or commitments. One application
with edition entitlements may be easier to maintain than separate
codebases. Licence expiry should not lock users out of their own saved
reports.

## Licensing decisions

Decide: - Per-device, per-user or business licence. - Subscription or
one-time purchase. - Offline grace period and activation recovery. -
Device transfer policy. - Refund/cancellation terms. - Behaviour when
offline or when a licence expires. - How local user data remains
accessible.

Do not prioritise licensing before core stability. Isolate licence logic
from diagnostics and repairs.

## Distribution

Potential channels include an official website, direct download,
technician/business sales and later a suitable marketplace. Investigate
code signing, installer trust, update integrity and publisher identity.

## Support and documentation

Plan an installation guide, first-run guide, beginner explanations,
diagnostic reference, repair risk notes, report/privacy guidance,
troubleshooting guide, release history and support process.

## Legal and operational review

Before selling: - Obtain appropriate advice on licence terms, consumer
rights, privacy and liability. - Define customer/device data collection
and purpose. - Provide a privacy notice where personal data is
processed. - Review third-party dependency licences. - Check product
name and branding availability. - Define vulnerability reporting and
security updates. - Ensure marketing claims match tested capabilities.

This is planning guidance, not legal advice.

## Commercial readiness checklist

-   [ ] Product name and branding reviewed.
-   [ ] Supported Windows versions documented.
-   [ ] Installation/uninstallation tested.
-   [ ] Code signing and distribution decided.
-   [ ] Licence model selected and reviewed.
-   [ ] Privacy and retention documented.
-   [ ] Third-party licences reviewed.
-   [ ] User documentation prepared.
-   [ ] Support/update process operational.
-   [ ] Critical/high defects addressed.
-   [ ] Usability tested with beginners, enthusiasts and technicians.
